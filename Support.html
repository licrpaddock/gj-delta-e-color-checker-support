螢幕錄製權限

App 需要 macOS「隱私權與安全性」中的螢幕錄製權限，才能讀取使用者主動框選的畫面像素。授權後請回到 App；若仍無法取樣，請結束並重新開啟 App。

取樣方式

點一下 L 或 R 預覽框可建立 30×30 pt 樣本，也可拖曳選擇自訂範圍。App 支援單區、三區與五區左右比較。

隱私

螢幕影像與色彩結果只在這台 Mac 上處理，不會儲存或上傳。請參閱 隱私政策。

Support

For version 0.1.10 (build 24)

For support, bug reports, or feature suggestions, contact:

Screen Recording permission

The app needs Screen Recording access in macOS Privacy & Security to read pixels in regions explicitly selected by the user. Return to the app after granting access. If sampling remains unavailable, quit and reopen the app.

Sampling

Click an L or R preview for a 30×30 pt sample, or drag to select a custom region. The app supports paired comparisons in 1, 3, and 5 Zones.

Privacy

Screen images and color results are processed only on this Mac and are never stored or uploaded. Read the Privacy Policy.
